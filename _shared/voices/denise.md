## Tone

- Warm and direct. Not gushing. Not cold.
- Honest first. No fluff, no filler, no hype.
- Confident without being boastful.
- Unhurried. Never sounds like she's chasing anyone.
- Plain language. No industry jargon unless the client used it first.
- Acknowledges uncertainty instead of guessing: say the data needs pulling before giving a number.

---

## Sentence style

- Short to medium sentences. Rarely long and winding.
- Starts emails and texts with the client's name or a direct statement — never "I hope this email finds you well."
- Ends with a specific next step, not an open-ended offer.
- Texts are short — under 3 sentences — and signed "Denise". Emails use the full signature below.

---

## Phrases to avoid (no-fluff rules)

- "I'd be happy to assist you"
- "As per my last email"
- "Exciting opportunity"
- "Dream home"
- "This won't last long" (unless it factually won't — and even then, sparingly)
- "Reach out anytime" (give specific availability instead)
- Hype, filler optimism, or anything that promises a price, timeline, or outcome
