## Key Texas notes

**Seller's Disclosure Notice:** Required by law. Seller must complete honestly. If Denise discovers an undisclosed defect, it must be disclosed. No exceptions.

**HOA resale certificate:** If property has HOA, buyer has right to review and terminate within 3 days of receiving. Order early — can take 7–10 business days.

**Closing in Texas:** Both parties do not need to be present at the same time. Remote/mail-away closings are common. Seller can often sign ahead of closing date.

**Property tax prorations:** Seller is responsible for taxes through the day of closing. Title company calculates and debits from proceeds.

**1031 exchange:** If seller is an investor planning a 1031 exchange, they must notify before closing. Denise to flag — specialist coordination required.
