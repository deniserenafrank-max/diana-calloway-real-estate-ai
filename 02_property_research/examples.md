## Example 1 — Mueller neighbourhood brief (buyer, family, $720k)

**Request (from 01_lead_qualifier):**
```
Requesting: 02_property_research
Client: James Rodriguez | Buyer | Pre-approved $720k
Profile: Dell relocation, family focus, timeline 90 days
Areas: Mueller (primary), Hyde Park (secondary)
Research type: Neighbourhood brief — showing prep
```

**Property research output:**

```
NEIGHBOURHOOD BRIEF — Mueller, Austin TX 78723
For: James Rodriguez | $720k budget | Family buyer | 90-day timeline

--- What Mueller is ---
Mueller is a 700-acre planned community built on the former Robert Mueller Airport site.
Developed from 2007 onward, it's one of Austin's most intentionally designed neighbourhoods:
grid streets, front porches, mixed-use town centre, parks, and a trail network.

It's the neighbourhood Austin's urban planners point to when they're defending density.
It's also HOA-governed. If James hasn't lived in an HOA, that's worth a conversation.

--- Who lives here ---
Young families, professionals, a significant Dell Medical School / UT Health employee
population. Walkability score is exceptional by Austin standards (Walk Score typically 75+).
The town centre has a HEB, restaurants, coffee, fitness — weekly errands on foot are real.

--- Price range at $720k ---
At $720k, James is in Mueller's solid mid-market:
- Townhomes: $580k–$750k range, some new builds at the top
- Single-family 3/2s: $650k–$850k on standard lots
- He has options. Not every option will have the yard he may expect from Dallas comparables.

⚠️ MLS CHECK NEEDED: Pull active Mueller listings in $650–750k range before showing.
Verify days-on-market — Mueller typically moves faster than broader Austin market.

--- School district ---
Austin ISD. Serves LBJ Early College High School via feeder pattern.
⚠️ VERIFY: Confirm current feeder school assignments — boundary changes have occurred.
Families who are UT-adjacent often supplement with private or magnet options.

--- HOA ---
Mueller HOA exists and is active. Fees typically $50–$150/month depending on property type.
Deed restrictions cover exterior modifications, landscaping standards, short-term rentals.
⚠️ MLS CHECK NEEDED: Confirm current HOA fees on any specific listing before quoting.

--- Commute to Dell Technologies (Round Rock campus) ---
Round Rock is ~25–30 min without traffic via I-35. With traffic, 40–55 min.
From Mueller, I-35 access is straightforward. Not a brutal commute for Texas.

--- Agent talking points for James ---
- "Mueller was built for exactly this — you've got the walkability Dallas neighbourhoods
  don't have, without the urban density that makes families nervous."
- "The HOA is real, but it's also why the neighbourhood looks the way it does."
- "At your budget you're competitive. Mueller doesn't sit — good properties move in 2–3 weeks."
- "Dell commute is about 25–30 minutes, which for Austin is genuinely good."

--- Hyde Park (secondary option) ---
If Mueller inventory is thin or James wants more lot size / older character:
Hyde Park offers 1890s–1920s bungalows, no HOA on most streets, strong UT-adjacent
walkability. Prices are wide ($600k–$950k+). Less new construction.
Trade-off: more character, less HOA order, older systems to inspect carefully.
```

## Example 2 — Listing prep (seller, 78745 South Austin, pricing opinion)

**Request (from 01_lead_qualifier via orchestrator):**
```
Requesting: 02_property_research
Client: The Garcias | Seller | 3/2 home, South Austin 78745
Property: approximately 1,600 sqft, built 2004, no HOA, original finishes
Goal: pricing opinion + competitive landscape before listing presentation
```

**Property research output:**

```
LISTING PREP BRIEF — South Austin 78745
For: The Garcias | Seller | 3/2, ~1,600 sqft, built 2004, original finishes

--- 78745 market snapshot ---
South Austin's 78745 sits between Slaughter Lane and William Cannon, with Garrison Park
and several greenbelt areas nearby. It's a working-class-to-professional neighbourhood
that has seen consistent appreciation but not the froth of East Austin or Barton Hills.

As of 2026, Austin is a buyer's market. Inventory is elevated — sellers who priced at 2021
numbers are sitting. This is the honest conversation the Garcias need to have.

--- Comp profile at 3/2, 1,600 sqft, original finishes ---
Properties matching this profile in 78745 have been selling in the:

High: $540k–$580k (updated kitchens, renovated baths, fresh exterior)
Mid: $480k–$530k (good condition, some updates, priced right)
Low: $430k–$470k (dated finishes, longer DOM, price reductions common)

The Garcias' original finishes put them in the mid-to-low range unless condition is excellent.

PRICING OPINION
Range: $480,000 – $525,000
Recommended list price: $505,000
Confidence: Medium
Basis: Comp profile for 78745 3/2s, original finishes, buyer's market discount
Verify: Pull 6 months of closed comps in 78745 for 3/2, 1,400–1,800 sqft before finalising

--- What can move the number ---
↑ Up: New HVAC or roof (<5 years), updated kitchen appliances, refinished floors
↓ Down: Deferred maintenance visible at showing, carpet throughout, dated bathrooms

--- Competitive landscape ---
⚠️ MLS CHECK NEEDED: Pull current active 3/2 competition in 78745 before the listing presentation.
Key question for agent: how many 78745 3/2s are sitting active right now and at what price?

--- Staging notes ---
Original finishes can work if the home is impeccable: deep clean, declutter aggressively,
neutral paint if possible, curb appeal investment (mulch, trim, fresh entry door). 
Buyers in 2026 are choosy. The home needs to compete, not just list.

--- Agent talking points for listing presentation ---
- "The market has corrected since 2022. I'm going to be straight with you about that."
- "Priced right, your home sells. Priced at 2021 numbers, we sit and do price reductions."
- "At $505k, you're competitive. You're not leaving money on the table — you're not leaving
  days on the market either, which costs you carrying costs and negotiating leverage."
```
