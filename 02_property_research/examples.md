Goal: pricing opinion + competitive landscape before listing presentation
Case file: 2026-004-denise-garcia.md
```

**Property research output:**

```

For: The Garcias | Seller | 3/2, ~1,600 sqft, built 2004, original finishes

--- Market context — Houston-north / Montgomery County ---
TO BE FILLED IN BY DENISE: current Conroe conditions (buyer's vs seller's market, inventory,

  High: updated kitchens, renovated baths, fresh exterior
  Mid:  good condition, some updates
  Low:  dated finishes, longer days on market, price reductions

PRICING OPINION
Range: $[low] – $[high]   ← fill from the HAR.com closed comps above
Recommended list price: $[specific]   ← fill once comps are pulled

Basis: Closed comps for similar 3/2s near the property, adjusted for original finishes

--- What can move the number ---
↑ Up: New HVAC or roof (<5 years), updated kitchen appliances, refinished floors
↓ Down: Deferred maintenance visible at showing, carpet throughout, dated bathrooms

--- Competitive landscape ---
⚠️ MLS CHECK NEEDED: Pull current active 3/2 competition near the property on HAR.com before

neutral paint if possible, curb appeal investment (mulch, trim, fresh entry door).
The home needs to compete, not just list.

--- Agent talking points for listing presentation ---
